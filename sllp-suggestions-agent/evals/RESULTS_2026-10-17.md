# Suggestions Agent v6 evaluation — 17 October 2026

The `suggestion-v6` change replaces irrelevant word-order fallbacks with
criterion-specific coaching. Direct example edits keep content words from the
composition; an example needing a new personal detail instead uses a visible
blank such as `[your feeling]`. The learner must replace that blank in an
editable revision. Advice can also be returned without an example.

The live Promptfoo run used **nine synthetic English cases, repeated three
times**: **27/27 passed (100%)**, with **zero assertion failures and zero
direct-agent errors**. Of the 27 case-runs, 24 returned Suggestions and three
correctly returned no priority improvement. Across the available responses,
45 suggestions were returned: 27 had an example or editable template, and 18
were advice-only. The new word-choice case produced the concise revision
“The referee blew the final whistle.” in all three runs.

The model was `gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18` at configured temperature 0.2.
Evaluation ID: `eval-2fT-2026-10-16T17:19:40` (UTC). Local results, ignored by
Git: `results/suggestions-v6-word-choice-repeat3.json` and
`results/suggestions-v6-word-choice-repeat3.html`.

Manual inspection found no clear new sensory details, invented outcomes, or
claimed feelings in the returned examples. The checks establish contract,
evidence, short example, and basic safety behavior on this small suite. They do
not establish general semantic accuracy or teaching usefulness. In particular,
the 100% pass rate cannot be compared directly with the older 75% result:
v6 allows advice-only responses and the suite now has an extra word-choice case.

After this live run, code review changed the fallback to retain a reviewer-approved
revision when it also passes the deterministic guard. The Suggestions unit suite
passes after that change (25 tests, 2 subtests), but the live Promptfoo run has
not been repeated. The 27/27 result describes the earlier v6 revision.
