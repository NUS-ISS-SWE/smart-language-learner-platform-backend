## Interpretation

The expanded suite shows that deterministic ranking and evidence attachment
worked on these synthetic cases, and the injection canaries did not appear in
the returned coaching. It also exposes a real limitation: a short example can
preserve the cited excerpt while inventing an important fact about the wider
story. A passing evidence check alone does not establish meaning preservation.

The outcome check is a narrow, case-specific string check. It does not detect
all unsupported implications, nor does the suite measure teaching quality.
Before making a broader claim, review anonymised learner compositions with
human raters and test additional kinds of meaning change. If the Suggestions
Agent is changed to address this failure, rerun the same suite and keep both
results for comparison.
